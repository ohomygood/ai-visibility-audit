隐私政策

我们收集什么

  • 你提交的店铺网址，以及我们从该公开网站只读抓取到的页面内容（首页、少量商品页、robots.txt、sitemap、llms.txt）。
  • 为限流而临时记录的 IP 地址（只保存在内存中，最多 1 小时）。
  • 付款时：Stripe 处理你的支付信息，我们只收到订单号、金额、邮箱和报告 ID，不接触银行卡号。付款链接会把报告 ID 和店铺域名交给 Stripe 一起保存，用于在 30 天内凭付款记录找回或重新生成你的报告。
  • 如果你给我们发邮件：邮件内容和邮箱地址。

我们怎么用

只用于生成你的体检报告和修复包、交付报告、处理退款和客服。不出售、不出租，也不用于广告。

第三方

  • AI 引擎（官方 API，如 OpenAI、Perplexity、Google Gemini）：我们只发送买家问题（含你的品牌名和品类），不发送你的个人信息。
  • Stripe：支付处理。
  • 托管：网站在 Cloudflare Pages，体检后端在 Render（美国/新加坡机房）。

保存多久

7 天。抓取数据、报告、修复包和客户邮件在生成后 7 天自动删除（每日自动清理任务）。请在 7 天内下载你的报告。法律或税务要求保存的付款记录由 Stripe 保存。

你的权利

你可以随时发邮件到 要求提前删除你的数据，我们会在 3 个工作日内处理。

Cookie

本站只在浏览器本地保存语言偏好，不使用跟踪 Cookie。

Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • The store URL you submit, and page content we fetch read-only from that public website (homepage, a few product pages, robots.txt, sitemap, llms.txt).
  • Your IP address, held in memory for at most one hour for rate limiting.
  • For payments: Stripe processes your payment details; we receive only the order id, amount, email and report id, never card numbers. The payment link passes the report id and your store's domain to Stripe, stored with the payment, so your paid report can be recovered or regenerated for 30 days.
  • If you email us: the message and your email address.

How we use it

Only to produce and deliver your audit and fix pack, handle refunds and support. We never sell or rent data or use it for advertising.

Third parties

  • AI engines (official APIs such as OpenAI, Perplexity, Google Gemini): we send buyer questions (including your brand name and category), never your personal data.
  • Stripe: payment processing.
  • Hosting: the website runs on Cloudflare Pages and the audit backend on Render.

Retention

7 days. Crawl data, reports, fix packs and customer emails are deleted automatically 7 days after creation by a daily cleanup job. Please download your report within 7 days. Payment records required for legal or tax purposes are kept by Stripe.

Your rights

Email at any time to have your data deleted early; we act within 3 business days.

Cookies

We store only your language preference locally in your browser. No tracking cookies.
