# Allbirds FAQ

### 1. What does Allbirds sell?
Allbirds offers the world’s most comfortable shoes, flats, and clothing made with natural materials like merino wool and eucalyptus. Our current product lineup includes shoes such as the Men's Cruiser Slip On, Men's Tree Runner, Women's Wool Runner Mizzles, Women's Wool Piper Go, Women's Allbirds Flip Flop, and Men's Canvas Runner NZ.

### 2. What materials are used to make your products?
Our products are made with natural materials like merino wool (such as ZQ Merino Wool used in our rain-ready sneakers) and eucalyptus (such as responsibly sourced eucalyptus tree fiber used in our breathable tree knit uppers).
